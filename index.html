FAQ

How is Moment different from other social media?

The only way to add someone is to take a Moment with them in person. And every post is two photos of the same moment, one from each friend's phone.

Why can't I just add friends?

You become friends by taking a Moment together. Next time you're with someone, take one.

Can I take a Moment with someone I'm not friends with yet?

Yes. Taking one is how you become friends.

Who can see my Moments?

The two of you, and each of your friends.

Does my friend need Moment too?

Yes, you both need it on your iPhones.

Is there an Android version?

It's coming soon.
